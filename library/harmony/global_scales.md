# Global Harmony & Scale Structure

## 1. Western Classical Harmony
*   **Functional Harmony:** Based on Tonic (I), Dominant (V), and Subdominant (IV) relationships.
*   **Counterpoint:** Independent melodic lines weaving together (e.g., Bach).
*   **Chromaticism:** Using notes outside the key for color (e.g., Debussy, Wagner).

## 2. Pentatonic Scales (East Asia & Blues)
*   **Structure:** 5 notes per octave. No half-steps (semitones) between adjacent notes.
*   **Formula:** 1 - 2 - 3 - 5 - 6 (relative to major).
*   **Characteristics:**
    *   **Ambiguity:** Lacks the strong leading tone (7th) and subdominant (4th) tension of Western scales.
    *   **Versatility:** The same pentatonic scale can often be used for both major and minor contexts.
    *   **Blues Connection:** The "Blues Scale" adds the "blue note" (b5) to the pentatonic minor.

## 3. Indian Classical (Raga System)
*   **Raga:** A melodic framework, not just a scale. It defines specific ascending (Arohana) and descending (Avarohana) patterns.
*   **Vadi/Samvadi:** The "King" and "Queen" notes of the Raga; the most important notes to emphasize.
*   **Microtones (Shruti):** Notes can be bent or held slightly sharp/flat for emotional effect.
*   **Drone:** Continuous background sound (Tanpura) on the tonic and dominant.

## 4. Middle Eastern & Arabic Maqam
*   **Quarter Tones:** Scales often include intervals smaller than a Western semitone (e.g., 3/4 tone).
*   **Hijaz Maqam:** Characterized by a augmented second between the 2nd and 3rd degrees.
*   **Improvisation:** Heavy reliance on ornamentation and melodic development over a static harmonic background.

## 5. West African & Polyrhythmic Harmony
*   **Gyil Scale:** A heptatonic scale used in West Africa, often with a unique tuning system.
*   **Harmonic Rhythm:** Harmony is often defined by the rhythmic interplay of instruments rather than chord changes.
*   **Call and Response:** A melodic phrase (call) is answered by a harmonic/rhythmic phrase (response).

## 6. Modes Summary

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ionian** | 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 | - | Bright, Happy |
| **Dorian** | 1 2 b3 4 5 6 b7 | **6 (Major)** | Mysterious, Bluesy |
| **Phrygian** | 1 b2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 | **b2** | Exotic, Dark |
| **Lydian** | 1 2 3 #4 5 6 7 | **#4** | Dreamy, Ethereal |
| **Mixolydian** | 1 2 3 4 5 6 b7 | **b7** | Rock, Bluesy |
| **Aeolian** | 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 | - | Sad, Natural Minor |
| **Locrian** | 1 b2 b3 4 b5 b6 b7 | **b5** | Unstable, Tense |
