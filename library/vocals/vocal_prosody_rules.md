# Vocal Prosody & Phonetic Stress Rules

## 1. Vocal Prosody
**Definition:** The set of acoustic properties that shape how speech sounds, including pitch, pace, stress, and pausing.

**Key Elements:**
*   **Pitch:** Variation in fundamental frequency (F0) to convey emotion and meaning.
*   **Pace:** Speed of delivery, affecting energy and clarity.
*   **Stress:** Emphasis on specific syllables or words.
*   **Pausing:** Strategic silences for breath and emphasis.

**Application for AI:**
*   **Natural Timing:** Avoid robotic, metronomic timing. Use slight variations in note duration and onset.
*   **Emotional Expression:** Map pitch and pace to emotional states (e.g., higher pitch for joy, slower pace for sadness).

## 2. Phonetic Stress Rules
**Definition:** The rules governing which syllables in a word are stressed.

**Rules:**
*   **Syllable Stress:** In English, stress usually falls on the first syllable of disyllabic words (e.g., "TA-ble") or the second syllable of trisyllabic words (e.g., "a-BOVE").
*   **Word Stress:** Content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives) are stressed more than function words (prepositions, articles).
*   **Sentence Stress:** Key words in a sentence are stressed to convey meaning.

**Application for AI Singing:**
*   **Syllable Alignment:** Ensure phonetic stress aligns with musical beats and accents.
*   **Vowel Lengthening:** Lengthen vowels on stressed syllables for clarity and expressiveness.
*   **Consonant Clarity:** Ensure consonants are crisp, especially on downbeats.

## 3. AI Singing Specifics
**TTS and Singing Models:**
*   **Prosody Control:** Modern TTS models allow control over pitch, pace, and stress.
*   **Emotion:** Prosody is key to conveying emotion in synthetic voices.
*   **Intonation:** Natural intonation patterns make AI voices sound more human.

**Best Practices:**
*   **Use Prosody Markers:** In your AI prompts, specify prosody markers (e.g., [stress], [pause]) to guide the AI.
*   **Layer Prosody:** Combine pitch, pace, and stress for a rich, expressive vocal performance.
