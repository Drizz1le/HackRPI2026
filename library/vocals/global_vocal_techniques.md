# Global Vocal Techniques & Singing Styles

## 1. Western Classical Techniques
*   **Bel Canto:** "Beautiful singing." Focus on smooth tone, agility, and high placement.
*   **Operatic:** Large volume, strong vibrato, and dramatic expression.
*   **Choral:** Blend and unison. Individual voices merge to create a collective sound.

## 2. Jazz & Blues Techniques
*   **Scat Singing:** Improvising with nonsense syllables (e.g., "doo-bee-doo").
*   **Swing:** Rhythmic feel where quarter notes are played with a long-short ratio.
*   **Blue Notes:** Flattening the 3rd, 5th, and 7th scale degrees for emotional effect.
*   **Vibrato:** A slight, rapid variation in pitch to add warmth.

## 3. Global Singing Styles

### A. Throat Singing (Mongolia/Tuvan)
*   **Technique:** Producing two pitches simultaneously (fundamental and overtone).
*   **Sound:** Ethereal, whistling quality.
*   **Application:** AI can simulate this by layering a harmonic oscillator above the main vocal track.

### B. Flamenco (Spain)
*   **Cante Jondo:** "Deep song." Raw, emotional, and gritty.
*   **Melisma:** Rapid succession of notes on a single syllable.
*   **Application:** Add slight pitch bends and a "raspy" texture to the AI voice.

### C. Gregorian Chant (Europe)
*   **Monophonic:** Single melodic line.
*   **Free Rhythm:** No strict meter; follows the natural flow of the Latin text.
*   **Application:** Use a slow, steady tempo with minimal vibrato.

### D. Bollywood (India)
*   **Hybrid Style:** Combines classical Indian techniques with Western pop structures.
*   **Expressive:** Wide dynamic range and emotional intensity.
*   **Application:** Use gamaka (ornamentation) and microtonal bends.

### E. African Polyphony
*   **Call and Response:** Leader and chorus alternate.
*   **Harmony:** Often uses parallel thirds and fourths.
*   **Application:** Layer multiple vocal tracks with slight timing variations to create a "human" feel.

## 4. AI Vocal Synthesis Guidelines
*   **Formant Shifting:** Adjust the "size" of the vocal tract to change the voice's character without changing the pitch.
*   **Breathiness:** Add breath noise to the beginning and end of phrases for realism.
*   **Micro-timing:** Slight variations in timing (humanization) prevent the "robotic" sound.
*   **Dynamic Range:** Ensure the AI voice can handle both soft and loud passages without clipping or losing detail.
