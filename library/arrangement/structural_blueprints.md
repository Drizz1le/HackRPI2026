# Song Structural Blueprints

## 1. EDM Structure
**Blueprint:**
*   **Intro:** 8-16 bars. Establishes rhythm and atmosphere.
*   **Breakdown:** 16-32 bars. Removes drums, focuses on melody and harmony.
*   **Build:** 8-16 bars. Increases energy with risers, snare rolls, and filter sweeps.
*   **Drop:** 16-32 bars. Full energy, main hook, heavy drums and bass.
*   **Outro:** 8-16 bars. Fades out or ends abruptly.

**Stems & Layering:**
*   **Drums:** Kick, snare, hi-hats, percussion. Layered for width and depth.
*   **Bass:** Sub-bass for low end, mid-bass for texture.
*   **Synths:** Lead, pad, arp, pluck. Panned for stereo image.
*   **Vocals:** Chops, phrases, hooks. Processed with reverb and delay.

**Energy Curve:**
*   Low (Intro) -> Medium (Breakdown) -> High (Build) -> Peak (Drop) -> Low (Outro).

## 2. Pop Structure
**Blueprint:**
*   **Verse:** 8-16 bars. Storytelling, lower energy.
*   **Pre-Chorus:** 4-8 bars. Builds tension, leads to chorus.
*   **Chorus:** 8-16 bars. Main hook, highest energy, memorable melody.
*   **Bridge:** 8-16 bars. Contrast, new chord progression or melody.
*   **Pattern:** ABABCB (Verse-Chorus-Verse-Chorus-Bridge-Chorus).

**Dynamics & Hooks:**
*   **Frequency Layering:** Verse focuses on mid-range, Chorus adds high and low frequencies.
*   **Hooks:** Melodic, rhythmic, or lyrical elements that repeat and catch the ear.

## 3. Symphonic Structure
**Blueprint:**
*   **Themes:** Main melodic ideas introduced in exposition.
*   **Development:** Themes are varied, combined, and modulated.
*   **Orchestration:** Full ensemble, dynamic shifts, timbral changes.
*   **Tension & Release:** Built through harmony, rhythm, and dynamics.

**Application:**
*   Use for cinematic or orchestral electronic tracks.
*   Layer strings, brass, and woodwinds for depth.
