# Research: Kanye West 2004-2011 production (chops, mix, arrangement)

Compiled from three research passes. Claims marked [UNVERIFIED] rest on one weak source or
estimate. BPM/key values are database estimates, not official.

## Chopping technique
| Track | Source / section | What was done |
|---|---|---|
| Through the Wire (2003) | Chaka Khan "Through the Fire", chorus vocal | coupled resample on the ASR-10, about +4 st (derived: 65.5 -> 83 BPM); the vocal is the hook |
| Touch the Sky (2005, Just Blaze) | Curtis Mayfield "Move On Up" horns | slowed/stretched on a Roland VP-9000, horns gated by the kick |
| Good Life (2007) | Michael Jackson "P.Y.T." | 127 -> 85 BPM time-stretch, -1 st, chopped on the ASR-10, MPC2000XL drums |
| Power (2010) | Continent No. 6 "Afromerica" chant + claps; King Crimson "21st Century Schizoid Man" | chant pitched up as the foundation, the Schizoid stab opens each section, Cold Grits break |
| Runaway (2010) | Rick James live "Look at ya!"; Backyard Heavies "Expo 83" drums | one-shot vocal on the MPC; break + added kick + reverbed clap |
| So Appalled (2010) | Lyn Collins "Think (About It)" "Woo!" | pitched up, distorted, on the offbeat every 4th count [UNVERIFIED placement] |
| Devil in a New Dress (2010, Bink!) | Smokey Robinson "Will You (Still) Love Me Tomorrow" | chopped and re-sequenced, pitch and tempo changed |
| Gorgeous (2010) | Enoch Light "You Showed Me" fuzz guitar | pitched down 1 semitone [UNVERIFIED, one source] |

- Main sampler: Ensoniq ASR-10. "The ASR-10 is like my left hand... I can chop samples into 61
  pieces" (Remix, Feb 2004). Drums on an Akai MPC2000XL.
- "I sample them at regular speed, then speed them up inside the ASR-10... A lot of old songs are
  too slow to rap on." Coupled resample is the default move.
- Rule of thumb [inference]: resample when the shift is 5 st or less, time-stretch when the tempo
  change would ruin the pitch.
- Reversed tails: no source found. Not presented as a Kanye trademark.

## Mix (numbers only where sourced)
- "Stronger" (Sound on Sound Inside Track, Manny Marroquin): 3 kick layers. The chorus kick gets
  +16 dB at 2 kHz and +17 dB at 14 kHz. The verse kick gets a Neve 1073 boost (+4 dB at 220 Hz)
  and 5:1 compression. Snare: +5 dB at 100 Hz, boost at 1.3 kHz, cut at 7 kHz. The sample is
  ridden ±6-7 dB by hand. The master bus dips 2-3 dB at 3 kHz.
- Kick "knock" at 800 Hz-1 kHz (Craig Bauer, Late Registration). Late Registration had all reverb
  removed: drums dry and close.
- Mike Dean: bass and synths on MBDTF, a Memory Moog daily, guitar solos.
- "No high strings, only low strings" (Anthony Kilhoffer on Kanye's MBDTF rule).
- MBDTF CD loudness (DR database): DR 5 album. Power DR5 / RMS -6.5; All of the Lights DR2 / -4.2;
  Runaway DR3 / -5.3; Devil DR6 / -7.3. Peaks clipped. Mastered by Vlado Meller.
- Recreation targets used here [UNVERIFIED starting points]:
  - Sample: HPF 100-150 Hz, LPF 10-12 kHz, -2.5 dB at 3 kHz.
  - Drum bus: 4:1, 15 ms attack, 100 ms release.
  - Master: -7 to -8 LUFS, ceiling -0.3 dBTP, clip-style rather than transparent.

## Arrangement
| Track | BPM | Key | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark Fantasy | 88 | F maj | 4:40 |
| Gorgeous | 92 | F# min | 5:57 |
| Power | 77-79 | C min | 4:52 |
| All of the Lights | 142 (feels 71) | Bb min | 5:00 |
| So Appalled | 85-87 | G# min | 6:38 |
| Devil in a New Dress | 80-83 | G#/Ab min | 5:52 |
| Runaway | 85-87 | E maj | 9:07 |
| Blame Game | 86 | F min | 7:49 |
| Lost in the World | 117 | F min | 4:16 |

- Runaway: a lone high E repeated 15 times on piano, then drums, cello and strings. A 3:06 coda of
  vocoded, overdriven wordless vocal.
- Devil in a New Dress: mid-song the beat drops out for about 90 s of live piano, strings and bass.
  Mike Dean's guitar solo peaks as the drums return.
- Power: King Crimson stab between verse blocks. A "beautiful death" outro that works as a bridge.
- Energy is built by adding layers. About two-thirds in, a drumless interlude resets it. The song
  ends on a long coda, not a fade on the hook.
- The template used for this flip (92 bars at 82 BPM): intro 8, verse 16, hook 8, verse 16, hook 8,
  interlude 8 (no drums), return 8 (solo peak), final hook 8, coda 12.
